Our Story

Beyond What Words Can Define.

ZAAYR began with a quiet question: why must a scent be explained before it can be loved? Our first fragrance carries no label for a mood, a season or a type of person. It is simply UNTITLED, meant to become whatever it is on your skin.

Every composition is built slowly and with restraint, so that it unfolds with you across the hours.
